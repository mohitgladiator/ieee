Global Vision & Legacy

What is IEEE MTT-S?

The IEEE Microwave Theory and Technology Society (MTT-S) is a global organization with more than 13,000 members and 190 chapters worldwide. It promotes the theory and applications of radio-frequency (RF), microwave, millimeter-wave, and terahertz technologies.

For over 70 years, MTT-S has driven advancements in wireless communications, radar systems, satellite navigation, medical imaging, and high-speed electronics, connecting global researchers, academic pioneers, and industry leaders.

About MITS-DU Chapter

Madhav Institute of Technology & Science - Deemed to be University, Gwalior (M.P.)

Our Mission

To foster technical excellence in RF, microwave, and electromagnetics among undergraduate and postgraduate students at MITS-DU by providing hands-on training on industry-standard CAD tools, organizing expert guest lectures, and encouraging student publications and hardware prototypes.

Our Vision

To establish MITS-DU Gwalior as a premier student hub for microwave engineering research and industrial applications, building strong bridges between academic learning, research laboratories, and top semiconductor & telecommunications industries.

Hands-on Workshops

Practical antenna fabrication and vector network analyzer (VNA) testing.

Simulation Software

Research Papers

Guidance on drafting and submitting papers to IEEE conferences.

Industrial Visits

Tours to satellite ground stations, defense labs, and RF manufacturing facilities.

Technical Wings

Domains & Focus Areas

Explore specialized technical domains guided by senior mentors and faculty advisors.

Antenna Design & Fabrication

Design microstrip patch antennas, reconfigurable arrays, planar UWB antennas, and mm-wave antennas for modern wireless platforms.

  • Microstrip Patch Antennas
  • MIMO Systems for 5G

RF & Microwave Circuit Design

Synthesis and analysis of passive and active RF components including filters, power dividers, low-noise amplifiers (LNA), and couplers.

  • RF Bandpass Filters
  • Impedance Matching Circuits

Electromagnetic Simulation

Mastering 3D electromagnetic solvers such as Ansys HFSS and CST Microwave Studio for high-frequency circuit verification.

  • S-Parameter Analysis
  • Far-field Radiation Patterns

Radar & Satellite Systems

Understanding radar target cross-section (RCS), phased array antennas, satellite microwave transponders, and Doppler radar modules.

  • FMCW Radar Prototyping
  • Satellite Earth Stations

Wireless & IoT Hardware

Integrating RF communication modules (LoRa, Zigbee, Wi-Fi 6) into smart IoT systems and wearable wireless sensors.

  • RF Energy Harvesting
  • Wearable Bio-Antennas

Student Research & Publications

Mentorship for drafting research manuscripts, presenting at IEEE conferences, and filing student utility patents.

  • IEEE Conference Submissions
  • Peer Review Writing Workshops
